# Legal review — Terms of Service

STATUS: PRODUCT DRAFT IMPLEMENTED — LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED BEFORE COMMERCIAL LAUNCH

The shared wording in `components/legal/terms-content.tsx` appears on `/terms`
and in the existing Dialog primitive. It covers the sixteen requested topics,
uses provider-neutral communications wording, describes payment recording without
claiming payment processing, and preserves rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.
This is a product draft, not a solicitor-approved agreement.

## Required business inputs

- Operating legal entity, company number (if applicable), and registered address.
- Governing law and jurisdiction, reviewed for the actual business and customers.
- Approved platform support and legal contact. No verified platform contact was
  found in the reviewed source/configuration. The public Contact section currently
  directs booking enquiries to the relevant business; it is not a substitute for
  a platform legal contact.
- **PRIVACY POLICY — PRE-LAUNCH FOLLOW-UP.** No policy URL is invented. Determine
  controller/processor roles, retention, subprocessors, customer rights and notices.
- Whether signup clickwrap or versioned acceptance is necessary later. This change
  deliberately adds no required checkbox, consent record, table or migration.
- Confirm the effective/updated date immediately before release. The draft uses
  8 October 2026; update it if publication occurs on another date.

No unresolved entity/address/jurisdiction placeholders are rendered publicly.
Legal review should assess the complete service and contract, not just liability
wording. Reference context includes [CCPC guidance on unfair terms](https://www.ccpc.ie/information-for-businesses/selling-goods-and-services/unfair-terms-in-contracts)
and [Consumer Rights Act 2022, section 71](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/eli/2022/act/37/section/71/enacted/en/html).
These references do not establish the platform's governing law.
